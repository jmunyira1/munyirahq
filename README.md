MunyiraHQ

A self-hosted personal finance and second-brain web app. Built for people who want a single, private place to track their accounts, plan their spending, and remember what they want — without relying on spreadsheets or scattered notes.

Inspired by Monica HQ but focused entirely on financial clarity rather than relationships.

✨ Key Features

Financial Accounts
Keep a record of all your financial accounts in one place — bank accounts, mobile money, savings, or any other account you hold. Get a clear picture of where your money sits at any given time.

Budget Allocations
Define how your money is divided across different areas of your life — rent, groceries, transport, savings, and so on. Allocations give you a structured view of your spending plan before money leaves your accounts.

Budget Items & Wishlist
Track specific things you want or plan to buy, each tied to an estimated cost. Whether it is a planned purchase this month or something you are saving toward over time, budget items keep your goals visible and grounded in real numbers.

Wants & Reminders
Capture general wants, notes, and reminders that do not fit neatly into a budget line. A lightweight memory layer that makes sure nothing slips through the cracks — from a product you spotted to a financial task you need to revisit.

Secure Authentication
All data is protected behind a secure login powered by CodeIgniter Shield. Sessions are managed safely and unauthenticated access to the dashboard is blocked automatically.
